Every copy of Your App ships with professional translations for 43 languages — built in, complete, and ready to use from day one. Users set their preferred language once, and the entire app interface, every label, button, menu, and system message, switches to match.

No translation work needed from you. No localization setup. No extra cost. It's included.

## How It Works

1. Open Your App and navigate to **Profile** → **Language Preferences**.
2. Tap the preferred language from the list.
3. The app switches to that language instantly, every screen, every label, every message.
4. The preference is saved and remembered, the user only needs to set it once.

<Tip>
  Language preference is remembered across sessions and app restarts. Users set it once and the app stays in their language automatically.
</Tip>
