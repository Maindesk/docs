The dashboard is the first thing users see when they open Your App, and it's designed to answer the most important question in three seconds: _how is my business doing right now?_

No digging through menus. No running reports. Just the numbers that matter, front and center.

## What the Dashboard Shows

- **Welcome banner** : greets the user by name so the app feels personal from the first tap
- **Revenue card** : total revenue for the current period with a trend indicator
- **Orders card** : total order count, tappable to jump directly to the orders list
- **Contacts card** : total contacts in the CRM, tappable to open the contacts list
- **Bookings card** : upcoming appointments, tappable to open the bookings list
- **Recent Orders** : the latest incoming orders with status, customer name, and amount
- **New Contacts** : the most recently added contacts
- **Upcoming Bookings** : appointments coming up soonest, so users can prepare

## How It Works

1. Open Your App, the dashboard loads immediately.
2. Read the four stat cards for a quick business health snapshot.
3. Tap any stat card to drill into the full list for that section.
4. Scroll down to see recent activity, new orders, contacts, and upcoming bookings.
5. Pull down anywhere on the screen to refresh all data instantly.

## Why It Matters

The dashboard removes the gap between "something happened in my business" and "I know about it." Most business owners check their phones first thing in the morning and between tasks throughout the day. The moment Your App is on their home screen, that check includes their business data, not just social media and email.

That daily touchpoint is what keeps users engaged with your platform and less likely to churn.
