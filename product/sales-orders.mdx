The Sales & Orders section gives users a complete view of their ecommerce activity — revenue trends, individual order details, and transaction status — all accessible from their phone the moment an order comes in.

## Key Capabilities

- **Revenue chart** — a visual line or bar chart showing earnings over the selected time period, so users can spot trends at a glance
- **Date filters** — quickly switch between Today, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, All Time, or a custom date range
- **Order cards** — each order displayed as a card with: customer name, order total, date, and a color-coded status pill (Pending, Processing, Completed, Cancelled, Refunded)
- **Tap to expand** — tapping an order card reveals full order details: line items, quantities, customer contact info, shipping address, and payment method
- **Real-time updates** — new orders appear as they come in, supported by push notifications that bring users directly to the relevant order

## How It Works

1. Open Your App and navigate to **Sales & Orders** from the side menu.
2. Use the date filter pills at the top to select a time period.
3. Read the revenue chart to understand your earnings trend.
4. Scroll through order cards to see recent transactions.
5. Tap any order to see its full details.
6. Receive a push notification the moment a new order is placed — tap it to open that order directly.

## Why It Matters

Orders are time-sensitive. A new order that sits unacknowledged for hours creates a poor customer experience and risks cancellations. With push notifications and a clear mobile view, your users can respond to every order within minutes of it being placed — from anywhere.
