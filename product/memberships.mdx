The Memberships section gives users a complete view of their membership plans and subscribers, who's signed up, on which plan, and since when, all accessible from the app with filters and QR sharing built in.

## Key Capabilities

- **Plans view** : see all active membership plans with name, price, billing cycle, and current subscriber count
- **Subscriber list** : browse individual members with their plan name, join date, and subscription status
- **Date filters** : filter memberships by join date range to analyze growth over a specific period
- **QR code generation** : generate a QR code for any membership plan that links directly to the signup page
- **Instant sharing** : share membership QR codes via any channel for in-person or digital promotion

## How It Works

1. Open Your App and navigate to **Memberships** from the side menu.
2. Browse the plans list to see which offerings are available and how many subscribers each has.
3. Tap into a plan to see individual subscribers on that plan.
4. Use date filters to narrow the view to a specific period.
5. Tap the **QR Code** button on any plan to generate a scannable signup link for sharing.

## Why It Matters

Memberships are recurring revenue, the most valuable kind. Staying on top of subscriber counts, plan performance, and new sign-ups helps users understand what's working and where to focus. Having that data on their phone means it influences decisions in real time, not just in monthly reviews.
