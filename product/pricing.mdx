## Additional Costs (Paid Directly to Apple and Google)

Two costs are required by Apple and Google for any app in their stores — these are charged directly by Apple and Google, not by Pixel Punch:

| Platform | Cost | Type |
|----------|------|------|
| Apple Developer Program | $99/year | Annual renewal |
| Google Play Developer Account | $25 | One-time registration fee |

<Note>
  These Apple and Google fees apply to **any** app published on their stores, regardless of who builds it. They are standard costs for any business with a mobile app.
</Note>

## Pricing Your App to Your Users

How you monetize Your App with your own users is entirely up to you. Common approaches include:

- **Include it in premium plans** — position the app as a reason to upgrade from entry-level to a higher tier
- **Charge a monthly add-on** — offer the app as an optional upgrade that users can add to any plan
- **Include it for all users** — differentiate your platform by giving every user a mobile app at no extra cost
- **Free trial, then paid** — let users experience the app for a period before converting them to a paid add-on

The app becomes a revenue-generating asset as soon as it's live. Most partners recover the platform fee quickly when even a fraction of their users adopt it.
