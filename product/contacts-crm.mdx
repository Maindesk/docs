The Contacts section is a full mobile CRM — every customer, lead, and subscriber in one searchable, filterable list that users carry with them everywhere. Whether following up on a lead at a networking event or pulling up a customer's history during a phone call, the answer is always one tap away.

## Key Capabilities

- **Full contact list** — every contact synced from the platform, searchable by name or email
- **Tag filter pills** — filter contacts instantly by tag (e.g., "VIP", "Lead", "Newsletter") to find the right group fast
- **Contact detail page** — tap any contact to see their full profile: name, email, phone, tags, subscriber list memberships, and complete order history
- **Order history in context** — see every purchase a contact has made without leaving their profile
- **Add new contacts** — create new contacts directly from the app, synced back to the platform in real time
- **Subscriber list membership** — see which email or marketing lists a contact belongs to

## How It Works

1. Open Your App and navigate to **Contacts** from the side menu.
2. Use the search bar to find a specific person, or browse the full list.
3. Tap a tag pill at the top to filter by contact segment.
4. Tap any contact card to open their full profile.
5. Scroll through their profile to see order history, tags, and list memberships.
6. Tap **Add Contact** (the \+ button) to create a new contact on the spot.

## Why It Matters

A CRM on a desktop is something you use when you're at your desk. A CRM on a phone is something you use when you need it — which is almost always when you're _not_ at your desk. Putting the complete customer database in your users' pockets means faster follow-ups, better client conversations, and fewer missed opportunities.
