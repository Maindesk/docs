Getting your branded app into the App Store and Google Play is a structured process with a clear timeline. Here's exactly what happens at each stage — what Pixel Punch handles, what we need from you, and when to expect each milestone.

<Tip>
  The biggest delay is almost always Apple Developer enrollment (24–48 hours). Start that process the moment you decide to move forward — before anything else.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Days 1–3: Setup & Configuration">
    **What we do:**
    - Collect your brand assets, platform credentials, and configuration details
    - Set up your Firebase project (your data, your project)
    - Configure the app with your brand colors, logos, and name
    - Connect to your platform's APIs and webhooks
    - Set up push notification certificates for iOS and Android

    **What you do:**
    - Complete the [onboarding checklist](/product/onboarding)
    - Enroll in the Apple Developer Program (if not already done)
    - Create your Google Play developer account (if not already done)
    - Invite Pixel Punch to both accounts

    **Milestone:** Configuration complete — your branded app is built and ready for testing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Days 4–7: Build & Internal Testing">
    **What we do:**
    - Run a full internal QA pass across iOS and Android
    - Test all integrations: orders, contacts, bookings, forms, push notifications, analytics
    - Verify white-label customization (your name, icon, colors, branding)
    - Fix any issues found during testing

    **What you do:**
    - Nothing at this stage — just wait for the TestFlight invite

    **Milestone:** App passes internal QA. Ready for your review.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Days 8–14: TestFlight / Beta Testing">
    **What we do:**
    - Upload the build to Apple TestFlight and Google Play Internal Testing
    - Send you an invite to test the app on your own device

    **What you do:**
    - Install the beta app via TestFlight (iOS) and the Play Console internal track (Android)
    - Test the app on real devices — log in, check your data, try all features
    - Report any issues or change requests

    **Milestone:** You've approved the build. Ready for App Store submission.

    <Warning>
      This is your chance to request changes before the app goes public. Review carefully — changes after App Store submission add time to the process.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Days 15–21: App Store Submission">
    **What we do:**
    - Prepare the complete App Store Connect submission: screenshots, description, keywords, privacy details
    - Prepare the Google Play Console submission
    - Submit both apps for review
    - Monitor the review process and respond to any Apple/Google review queries

    **What you do:**
    - Approve the store listing text and screenshots before submission
    - Be available to respond quickly if Apple asks a clarifying question

    **Review times:**
    - Apple App Store: typically 1–3 business days for a new app
    - Google Play: typically 1–3 days for a new app, sometimes faster

    **Milestone:** Both apps approved and ready for release.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Days 22–30: Launch & Handoff">
    **What we do:**
    - Coordinate release timing with you
    - Publish both apps to the App Store and Google Play
    - Monitor for any post-launch issues
    - Provide you with the App Store and Play Store links for your website and marketing
    - Hand over admin access and documentation

    **What you do:**
    - Share the app links with your users
    - Announce the launch through your channels

    **Milestone:** Your App is live. Your users can download it. 🎉
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After Launch

Pixel Punch doesn't disappear after your app goes live. Ongoing support includes:

- **Over-the-air updates** — new features and improvements are delivered automatically to all users without App Store re-submission
- **Platform compatibility** — we maintain compatibility with iOS and Android OS updates as they're released
- **Bug fixes** — if something breaks, we fix it
- **New features** — as we add capabilities to the platform, your app gets them

Your app keeps improving without any additional effort from you.
