The Bookings section gives users a clear, organized view of every appointment — past, present, and future — so they can manage their schedule and prepare for upcoming meetings without needing to be at a computer.

## Key Capabilities

- **Status pills** — appointments are grouped and labeled by status: **Upcoming**, **Today**, and **Completed**, making it immediately clear what needs attention
- **Date filters** — filter bookings by specific date ranges to find historical appointments or look ahead at upcoming weeks
- **Booking cards** — each booking displayed with: client name, service or appointment type, date and time, and status
- **Expandable details** — tap any booking card to expand it and see full details: client contact information, notes, location (if applicable), and any custom fields from the booking form
- **Real-time updates** — new bookings appear as they're made, backed by push notifications

## How It Works

1. Open Your App and navigate to **Bookings** from the side menu.
2. The default view shows all upcoming and today's appointments at the top.
3. Use the date filter to look back at past bookings or forward at a future week.
4. Tap any booking card to expand and see the full appointment details.
5. Receive a push notification the moment a new booking is made — tap it to open that booking directly.

Service businesses live and die by their calendar. Missing a new booking or arriving unprepared for an appointment costs money and damages client relationships. With Your App, users know about new appointments the moment they're made and have every detail they need — client info, notes, timing — before they walk in the door.
