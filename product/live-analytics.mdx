The Live Analytics section turns Your App into a real-time window into website traffic — showing exactly who's visiting, where they're coming from, what they're doing, and how that compares to previous periods. Powered by Google Analytics 4, it brings data that used to require a laptop and a browser tab to your pocket.

## What You See in Real Time

- **Live visitor count** — the exact number of people on the website right now
- **World map with live dots** — every active visitor represented as a pulsing dot on a world map, updating in real time
- **Device breakdown** — see the split between desktop, mobile, and tablet visitors at a glance
- **Top countries** — a ranked list of visitor countries with flags, showing where traffic is actually coming from
- **Live activity feed** — a scrolling log of page views and events as they happen

## Historical Reporting

Alongside real-time data, the analytics section includes historical reporting with period filters (Today, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, Last 90 Days):

- **Sessions chart** — a line chart showing traffic volume over the selected period
- **Traffic sources** — where visitors came from: Direct, Organic Search, Social Media, Referral, Email
- **Top pages** — the most-visited pages ranked by sessions
- **Countries table** — all countries with visitor counts, sorted by volume
- **Devices donut chart** — a visual breakdown of device types used by visitors

<Note>

</Note>

## Why It Matters

Most platform users have no idea how much traffic their website is getting, where it's coming from, or whether anything they're doing is working. Putting a live traffic view on their phone changes that completely. It makes website performance a daily habit instead of an occasional curiosity — and that engagement keeps users invested in their platform.
