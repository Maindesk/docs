Every contact form, quote request, consultation inquiry, and survey response that comes through a user's website shows up in the Forms section of Your App — organized, tracked, and flagged when unread. No more leads going cold because they sat in an inbox overnight.

## Key Capabilities

- **Submission cards** — each form submission displayed as a card with the form name, submission date/time, and the submitter's name or email
- **Unread indicators** — new, unread submissions are highlighted with a blue dot and a **NEW** pill so they stand out immediately
- **Read/unread tracking** — once a submission is tapped and viewed, it's automatically marked as read; the unread count in the navigation decreases accordingly
- **Expandable field data** — tap any submission card to expand it and see every field the visitor filled out — name, email, phone, message, and any custom fields
- **Push notifications** — users receive an instant push notification for every new form submission, with the notification tapping directly to that submission

## How It Works

1. Open Your App and navigate to **Forms** from the side menu.
2. New submissions are marked with a blue dot and NEW pill — these need your attention first.
3. Tap any submission card to expand it and read the full content.
4. The submission is automatically marked as read once opened.
5. Receive a push notification for every new submission — tap to go directly to it.
