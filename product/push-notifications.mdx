Push notifications are the backbone of what makes Your App genuinely useful — not just a mobile view of a desktop dashboard, but an active business intelligence tool that reaches users the moment something important happens.

Email has a 20% open rate. Push notifications average **90%**. When a new order comes in at 9pm, your user knows about it at 9pm.

## Why Push Notifications Change Everything

Most business owners don't check their management platform constantly throughout the day. They check it when they remember, when they have a moment, or when they're at their computer. That means orders sit unacknowledged, leads go cold, and bookings get missed.

Push notifications flip this entirely. Instead of users going to find information, the information comes to them — in real time, on the device they carry everywhere.

## Notifications for Users

Users receive push notifications for every important event on their websites and accounts:

| Event | What It Means |
|-------|--------------|
| 🛒 **New Order** | A customer has placed an order — includes order value and customer name |
| 👤 **New Contact** | A new contact has been added or signed up |
| 📅 **New Booking** | A new appointment has been scheduled |
| 📋 **Form Submission** | A visitor has submitted a contact or lead form |
| 🏷️ **New Member** | A new subscriber has joined a membership plan |

## Notifications for Admins

Platform administrators receive notifications for platform-level events:

| Event | What It Means |
|-------|--------------|
| 👋 **New User** | A new user has registered on the platform |
| ✅ **New Subscription** | A user has subscribed to a paid plan |
| 🔄 **Subscription Renewed** | A user's subscription has auto-renewed |
| ⏰ **Trial Expiring** | A trial account is about to expire — time to reach out |
| ❌ **Trial Expired** | A trial has ended without converting |
| 🗂️ **New Project** | A new project has been created on the platform |
| 💬 **Live Chat Message** | A website visitor has sent a chat message |

## Fully Customizable

Users control exactly which notifications they want. From the **Notification Preferences** screen, every notification type can be individually toggled on or off. Users who only care about orders and bookings can silence everything else without losing what matters to them.

## Custom Sounds Per Event Type

Different event types use different notification sounds — so users know what kind of event happened before they even look at their phone. A new order sounds different from a new chat message. Familiarity builds over time until the sound alone is enough.

## Smart Navigation

Tapping a notification doesn't just open the app — it opens the correct page for that event. Tap a "New Order" notification and you land directly on that order's detail page. If the user manages multiple websites, the app automatically switches to the correct website context before opening the notification content.
