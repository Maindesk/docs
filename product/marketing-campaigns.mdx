The Marketing & Campaigns section gives platform admins the tools to reach their entire user base, or targeted segments, with push notifications, news articles, and automated triggered messages. No email tools needed. No third-party marketing platforms. Just a direct line to every user's phone.

## Key Capabilities

**Push Campaigns**

- Create and send campaigns with a title, message body, and optional deep link
- Target all users or a specific audience segment
- Deep link recipients to any page inside the app when they tap the notification
- Track campaign performance: messages sent, opened, clicked, and converted

**News & Announcements**

- Publish news articles delivered directly to users' apps
- Use for product launches, feature announcements, platform updates, or policy changes
- Articles are visible in the app's news feed and delivered as notifications

**Automation Rules**

- Set up triggered notifications that fire automatically based on user or platform events
- Example triggers: trial about to expire, subscription renewed, new user registered
- Reduce manual work for routine communications that need to go out reliably every time

## How It Works for Push Campaigns

1. Navigate to **Marketing** in the admin section of Your App.
2. Tap **New Campaign**.
3. Enter a title and message (keep it concise, push notifications have character limits for a reason).
4. Choose an audience: All Users or a specific segment.
5. Optionally add a deep link, the page inside the app the user lands on when they tap.
6. Tap **Send Now** to deliver immediately, or **Save as Draft** to schedule for later.
7. View campaign stats after sending: sent count, open rate, click rate, and conversions.

## Why It Matters

Marketing to users through their phone, with their explicit permission, via an app they chose to install, is fundamentally different from email. Messages arrive in a context of high attention, users are predisposed to engage, and the path from notification to action is one tap. Campaigns sent through Your App should be used thoughtfully and reserved for genuinely valuable communications, and when they are, they perform exceptionally.
