## What Gets Customized

Every visible element of the app carries your brand:

**App Identity**

- App name (what appears below the icon on the home screen)
- App icon (your logo, your design, your style)
- Splash screen (the screen shown while the app loads, fully branded)
- Drawer/sidebar logo (your logo appears in the navigation menu)

**Visual Design**

- Primary brand color, applied to every button, accent, highlight, and interactive element throughout the app. One color value, consistent everywhere.
- Login screen, branded with your logo and color scheme so the first impression is yours

**App Store Presence**

- App name on the App Store and Google Play
- App Store and Google Play listing description, subtitle, and keywords, your words
- Published under **your** Apple Developer account and Google Play developer account, your company name appears in the store listing, not ours
- Screenshots and preview images (we help you create these)

## Published Under Your Developer Accounts

This is the part that matters most for perception. When users search for or find Your App on the App Store or Google Play, they see:

- **Your app name**
- **Your company name** as the developer
- **Your branding** in the screenshots and description

Not "Pixel Punch." Not "Powered by Pixel Punch." Your company, full stop.
