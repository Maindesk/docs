The Products section gives users access to their full product catalog from their phone, so they can browse, reference, and share products with customers in any context, whether they're in a store, at an event, or having a conversation.

## Key Capabilities

- **Full product list** : every product from the platform catalog displayed with name, image, price, and status
- **Product details** : tap any product to see the full description, variants, pricing, and availability
- **QR code generation** : generate a scannable QR code for any product that links directly to its purchase page on the website
- **Instant sharing** : share the QR code as an image via any channel (WhatsApp, SMS, email) so customers can go straight to checkout

## How It Works

1. Open Your App and navigate to **Products** from the side menu.
2. Browse or search for the product you want.
3. Tap a product to open its detail page.
4. Tap the **QR Code** button to generate a scannable code for that product's URL.
5. Share the QR code image directly from the share sheet, via message, email, or any app.

  QR codes are perfect for in-person selling, at markets, pop-up shops, trade shows, or consultations. Instead of typing a URL, customers scan and they're straight to the product page.

## Why It Matters

The gap between "a customer expresses interest" and "that customer visits the product page" is where sales are lost. QR codes close that gap to a single scan. Your App turns every user's phone into a mobile sales tool, their product catalog and a direct path to purchase, always ready.
