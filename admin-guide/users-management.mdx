The Users Management section gives you a complete view of every user registered on your platform, their account status, subscription details, and activity, all in one searchable list.

## The Users List

Each user appears as a card showing:

- **Full name** and email address
- **Account status** : color-coded badge indicating their current state
- **Plan name** (if on a paid plan)
- **Registration date**
- **Last activity** (when they last logged in or took an action)

## User Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | User is on a paid plan and their subscription is current |
| **Trial** | User is in a free trial period |
| **Trial Expiring** | User's trial ends within the next 7 days needs attention |
| **Expired** | User's trial ended without converting or subscription lapsed |
| **Cancelled** | User cancelled their subscription |

The status badges use consistent colors throughout the app so you can scan the list quickly.

## Filtering Users

Use the filter options at the top of the Users list to narrow by:

- Status (Active, Trial, Expired, etc.)
- Plan (if you have multiple subscription tiers)
- Registration date range

Filtering is useful for identifying groups that need action, for example, filtering by "Trial Expiring" shows you exactly who to reach out to today.

## User Detail Page

Tap any user to open their full profile:

- Full contact information
- Subscription history (all plans, start and end dates)
- Projects and websites on their account
- Recent activity log
