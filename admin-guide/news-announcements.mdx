News & Announcements lets you publish content that is delivered to your users as notifications and available to read inside Your App. Use it for platform updates, feature launches, policy changes, tips, or any content you want your user base to see.

## How News Differs from Push Campaigns

| Feature | Push Campaign | News Article |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Format | Short notification only | Full article with title, body, and images |
| Persistence | One-time notification, no stored content | Stored in the app's news feed and accessible anytime |
| Best for | Time-sensitive calls to action | Informational content, updates, evergreen guides |
| Delivery | Push notification | Push notification \+ in-app news feed |

## Creating a News Article

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to News">
    Open the side menu, tap **Marketing**, then tap **News & Announcements**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap New Article">
    Tap **\+ New Article** to open the article creation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the headline">
    Enter a clear, informative headline. This is what users see in the notification and at the top of the article.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the article body">
    Write the full content of the article. You can include formatted text, lists, and links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a cover image (optional)">
    Upload an image to appear at the top of the article. A clear, relevant image improves engagement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Tap **Publish** to send the article immediately. Users receive a push notification and the article appears in their in-app news feed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use Cases

- **New feature launches** : announce a new capability and explain how to use it
- **Platform maintenance** : let users know about scheduled downtime in advance
- **Tips and tutorials** : share best practices for getting the most out of the platform
- **Policy updates** : communicate changes to terms, pricing, or policies
- **Success stories** : feature a user success story to inspire and retain the broader base

<Tip>
  News articles that provide genuine value, tips, tutorials, feature guides, tend to be the most read and appreciated. Reserve announcements for content that users will actually want to read.
</Tip>
