A push campaign is a direct message sent to your users' phones, a push notification that you create, target, and send from within the app. It's the fastest way to reach your entire user base or a specific segment with an announcement, promotion, or important update.

## Creating a Campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Marketing">
    Open the side menu and tap **Marketing**, then tap **Push Campaigns**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap New Campaign">
    Tap the **\+ New Campaign** button to open the campaign creation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the title">
    Enter a short, clear title for the notification. This is the bold headline users see first. Keep it under 50 characters, notifications get truncated on some devices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    Enter the notification body, the main message text. Be specific and clear about what you're communicating. Keep it under 150 characters to ensure it displays fully on all devices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your audience">
    Select who receives this campaign:

    - **All Users** : everyone with notifications enabled
    - **Active Subscribers** : paid plan users
    - **Trial Users** : users currently in a trial
    - Other available segments for your platform
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a deep link (optional)">
    Choose where users land when they tap the notification:

    - Leave blank to open the app home screen
    - Select a specific app section (Orders, Bookings, Analytics, etc.)
    - Enter a custom URL to send users to a web page
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send or save as draft">
    - Tap **Send Now** to deliver the campaign immediately to all selected recipients
    - Tap **Save as Draft** to save without sending : you can edit and send later
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Campaign Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Created but not yet sent, can be edited and sent at any time |
| **Sending** | Currently being delivered to recipients |
| **Sent** | Delivery complete |
| **Scheduled** | Set to send at a future time (if scheduling is enabled) |

## Per-Campaign Stats

After a campaign is sent, tap it in the campaign list to see its performance:

| Metric | What It Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Sent** | Total number of notifications delivered |
| **Opened** | How many users opened the app from this notification |
| **Clicked** | How many users tapped the notification (same as opened for push) |
| **Converted** | Users who completed a target action after tapping |

Use these stats to understand what messages resonate with your users and refine future campaigns.

<Tip>
  The best-performing push campaigns are short, specific, and include a clear reason to tap. "Your trial ends in 48 hours, upgrade now" outperforms "Important update regarding your account" every time.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Push campaigns are visible to users on their lock screens. Use them for genuinely useful or important communications. Campaigns that feel like spam will lead users to disable notifications, which reduces the value of the feature for everyone.
</Warning>
