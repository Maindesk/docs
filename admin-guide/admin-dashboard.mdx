The Admin Dashboard is the command center for your white-label platform. It brings together everything you need to understand your platform's health in one screen, who's on your users' websites right now, how your revenue is trending, what your trial pipeline looks like, and what's happening across your user base.

## Live Visitors Map

At the top of the Admin Dashboard is a real-time world map showing active visitors across all your users' websites simultaneously.

- **Pulsing dots** represent visitors currently on a website — each dot's location reflects the visitor's geographic region
- **Live visitor count** shows the exact number of people currently browsing your users' websites
- **Device breakdown** — a quick visual showing the proportion of visitors on desktop, mobile, and tablet
- **Top countries** — the top visitor countries ranked by current active sessions, each with a country flag
- **Live activity feed** — a scrolling log of page views and events happening in real time across all sites

<Tip>
  The live map is a compelling feature to show during sales demos. Watching visitor dots appear around the world in real time demonstrates the scale and reach of your platform instantly.
</Tip>

## Users & Projects Cards

Below the live map, two summary cards give you platform-level counts:

- **Users** — total registered users on your platform, tappable to open the full Users Management section
- **Projects** — total projects across all users, tappable to open Projects Management

## Platform Revenue

The revenue section shows your key subscription metrics:

- **MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)** — your total contracted monthly subscription income
- **Active Subscriptions** — the number of users on paid plans right now
- **Renewals This Month** — subscriptions that have already renewed in the current billing period
- **Churned** — subscriptions that cancelled or did not renew this period

These metrics together tell the story of your platform's financial health: MRR for scale, churn for retention, renewals for momentum.

## Trials Section

The trials section gives you real-time visibility into your conversion pipeline:

- **Active Trials** — users currently in a trial period
- **Trials Expiring Soon** — trials ending within the next 7 days — these need attention now
- **Expired Trials (Unconverted)** — trials that ended without converting to a paid plan

The "Trials Expiring Soon" list is actionable — these are the users most likely to convert with a timely message or outreach. Consider using automation rules or a manual push campaign to reach them before they expire.

## Platform Activity

A chronological activity feed showing recent events across the platform:

- New user registrations
- New subscriptions and renewals
- Trial starts and expirations
- New project creations
