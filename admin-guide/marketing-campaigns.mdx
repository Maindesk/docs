The Marketing section gives you three tools for reaching your users directly through their phones: push campaigns for targeted one-time broadcasts, news articles for ongoing content, and automation rules for triggered communications that run on their own.

## Campaign Stats Overview

The main Marketing screen shows a summary of your recent campaign activity:

- **Total campaigns sent** in the current period
- **Overall open rate** across all campaigns
- **Overall click rate** (users who tapped a campaign notification)
- **Conversion rate** (users who completed a desired action after tapping)

Individual campaign cards below the summary show per-campaign stats, so you can see which messages resonated and which didn't.

## Audience Targeting

When creating a push campaign, you choose who receives it:

- **All Users** : every user on your platform who has notifications enabled
- **Active Subscribers** : users currently on a paid plan
- **Trial Users** : users in a free trial period
- **Specific Segments** : groups defined by custom criteria (varies by platform configuration)

Targeting ensures you're sending relevant messages to the right people, rather than blasting everyone with every communication.

## Deep Link Options

When a user taps a push campaign notification, you can send them to:

- **The app home screen** (default)
- **A specific section** : Orders, Bookings, Analytics, etc.
- **A specific URL** : useful for pointing users to a web page, a blog post, or a landing page outside the app

Deep links make campaigns more actionable : instead of just opening the app, users land exactly where you want them.
