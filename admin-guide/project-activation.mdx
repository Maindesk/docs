Project activation is the process of moving a project from trial (or expired) status to a paid subscription plan. It can be done directly from Your App in a few taps, without needing to access a desktop or admin panel.

## How to Activate a Project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the project">
    Navigate to **Projects Management** in the admin section. Use the status filter to find Trial or Expired projects, or search for the specific project by name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the project detail">
    Tap the project card to open its full detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Activate">
    Tap the **Activate** button on the project detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a plan">
    A list of available subscription plans loads from your platform's API. These are the live plans from your pricing configuration, not hardcoded options. Select the plan the user wants to subscribe to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the billing cycle">
    Select **Monthly** or **Yearly** billing for the selected plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm activation">
    Review the plan name and billing cycle, then tap **Confirm**. The project is activated immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  ![Project Activation flow showing the plan selection screen and billing cycle picker](/images/admin-guide/project-activation.png)

## What Happens After Activation

Once you confirm activation:

- The project status changes from **Trial** or **Expired** to **Active** immediately
- The user receives a push notification confirming their subscription is active
- The project's trial end date is replaced with the subscription start date
- The subscription appears in your platform revenue metrics

## Plans Are Fetched Live from Your Platform

The list of plans shown during activation comes directly from your platform's API, not from hardcoded values in the app. This means:

- New plans you add to your platform appear automatically
- Plans you remove or deactivate no longer appear
- Pricing changes are reflected immediately

There's nothing to update in the app when you change your plan offerings.

<Tip>
  Activation is most useful when you've spoken with a user personally and they've agreed to subscribe verbally or via message, you can activate their account on the spot during the call without needing to send them to a checkout page.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Project activation creates a real subscription record in your platform. Double-check the plan name and billing cycle before confirming, activation cannot be undone from within the app and may require manual intervention in your platform's billing system to reverse.
</Warning>
