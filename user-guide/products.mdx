The Products section gives you access to your full product catalog from your phone, with the ability to generate a shareable QR code for any product in seconds.

## What You'll See

**Product List**

All products from your platform catalog, displaying:

- Product image (thumbnail)
- Product name
- Price
- Status (available, out of stock, etc.)

**Product Detail**

Tap any product to see:

- Full description
- All variants (sizes, colors, options)
- Price and availability
- A **QR Code** button

**QR Code**

Tap the QR Code button on any product to generate a scannable QR code that links directly to that product's page on your website. You can share the QR code as an image via any app, WhatsApp, Messages, email, or save it to your camera roll.

## How to Use Products

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Products">
    Open the side menu and tap **Products**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the product">
    Browse the catalog or use the search bar to find a specific product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the product detail">
    Tap the product card to see its full details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate a QR code">
    Tap the **QR Code** button to generate a scannable code for that product's URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the QR code">
    Tap the Share button to send the QR code image via any app, or save it to your phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  QR codes are perfect for in-person situations, markets, events, trade shows, or consultations. Instead of giving someone a URL to type in, they scan and they're on your product page in seconds.
</Tip>

## Use Cases for Product QR Codes

- **At a market or pop-up shop** : customers scan to see full product details and purchase online
- **On printed materials** : add the QR code to a business card, flyer, or product packaging
- **During a presentation or consultation** : share a product link instantly without searching for a URL
- **Via WhatsApp or SMS** : send the QR code image to a customer who's ready to buy

<Warning>
  Products are read-only in the app — you can view and share them, but editing product details, pricing, or inventory must be done through your platform's web dashboard.
</Warning>
