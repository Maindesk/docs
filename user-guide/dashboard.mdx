The dashboard is the first screen you see when you open Your App. It's designed to give you an instant read on how your business is doing, without tapping into any specific section.

## What You'll See

**Welcome Banner**

A personalized greeting with your name at the top. This confirms you're logged into the right account and gives the app a personal feel.

**Stat Cards**

Four tappable cards showing your key business metrics:

- **Revenue** : your total earnings for the current period
- **Orders** : total number of orders received
- **Contacts** : total contacts in your CRM
- **Bookings** : upcoming appointments

Each card is tappable, tap any card to jump directly to the full list for that section.

**Recent Orders**

A scrollable list of your most recent orders, showing customer name, order amount, and status. This updates in real time as new orders come in.

**New Contacts**

The most recently added contacts, useful for following up with people who signed up or were added recently.

**Upcoming Bookings**

Your next appointments in chronological order, so you can see at a glance what's coming up today and this week.

## How to Use the Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your App">
    The dashboard loads automatically when you open the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the stat cards">
    Check Revenue, Orders, Contacts, and Bookings for a quick business health snapshot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap any stat card">
    Tap a card to go directly to the full list, for example, tap Orders to see all orders, or tap Bookings to see your appointment calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll down for recent activity">
    Scroll below the stat cards to see recent orders, new contacts, and upcoming bookings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pull to refresh">
    Pull down from the top of the screen to refresh all data and load the latest information.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Pull down on the dashboard to refresh all data instantly, useful after a busy morning when you want to see the latest numbers without navigating away.
</Tip>

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why are some cards showing zero?** If a stat card shows zero, it means there's no data for that section yet, or no data connected to the currently selected website. Use the website selector at the top to check you're viewing the right website.

**How often does the data update?** The dashboard shows the latest data from your platform. Tap a stat card or pull down to refresh at any time.

**Can I customize what appears on the dashboard?** The dashboard layout is fixed and shows the most universally useful information. Notification preferences can be customized in Profile → Notification Preferences.
