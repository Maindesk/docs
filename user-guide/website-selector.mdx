If your platform account includes multiple websites, funnels, or projects, the website selector lets you switch between them and view each one's data independently, all from within the same app.

## Where to Find It

The website selector appears at the top of the screen, just below the header. It shows the name of the currently active website. Tap it to open the full list of your websites and switch to a different one.

## How Websites Are Ordered

Websites appear in the selector in a consistent order:

1. **Main website first** : your primary website appears at the top
2. **Custom domain websites** : websites with a custom domain follow
3. **Subdomain/funnel websites** : remaining sites appear after

This ordering ensures your most important website is always first and easiest to select.

## Previewing a Website

Each website in the selector has a small **eye icon** (preview button) next to its name. Tap the eye icon to open that website in your phone's browser for a quick preview, without switching your active website in the app.

## Smart Notification Switching

When you tap a push notification for an event on a specific website (an order, booking, or form submission), Your App automatically switches to that website's context before opening the notification content. You don't need to manually switch websites before viewing the notification.

This means:

- A "New Order" notification from Website B will switch you to Website B's orders, even if you were last looking at Website A
- When you close the notification detail, you're on Website B, tap the website selector to switch back if needed

## How to Switch Websites

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the website selector">
    Tap the website name shown at the top of the screen. A modal slides up showing all your websites.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse your websites">
    Scroll through the list to find the website you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a website to switch">
    Tap any website name to switch to it. All data in the app, dashboard, orders, contacts, bookings — immediately reflects that website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swipe down to close without switching">
    If you opened the selector by accident or just wanted to preview, swipe down on the modal to close it without changing your active website.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you primarily use one website, you'll rarely need to touch the selector. It's most useful for agencies, freelancers, or business owners managing multiple separate web properties from a single platform account.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  When you switch websites, all sections of the app (dashboard, orders, contacts, bookings, forms) update to show that website's data. Make sure you're on the right website before reviewing or acting on any information.
</Warning>
