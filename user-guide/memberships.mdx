The Memberships section gives you a clear view of your membership plans and subscribers — who's signed up, when they joined, and how each plan is performing.

## What You'll See

**Plans View**

Your active membership plans, each showing:
- Plan name
- Price and billing cycle (monthly, yearly, etc.)
- Number of active subscribers

**Subscriber List**

Tap a plan to see individual subscribers, including:
- Subscriber name and email
- Join date
- Current subscription status

**Date Filters**

Filter the subscriber list by join date range — useful for reviewing growth over a specific period.

**QR Code Sharing**

Each plan has a QR Code button. Tap it to generate a scannable code that links directly to that plan's signup page — ready to share at events, in messages, or on printed materials.

## How to Use Memberships

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Memberships">
    Open the side menu and tap **Memberships**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse your plans">
    See all active plans with their current subscriber counts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a plan for subscribers">
    Tap a plan card to see the list of subscribers on that plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by date">
    Use the date filter to narrow subscribers to those who joined in a specific period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share a plan via QR code">
    Tap the **QR Code** button on any plan to generate and share a signup link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Generate a QR code for your most popular membership plan before attending a networking event or speaking engagement — you can share it instantly with anyone who's interested.
</Tip>

## Getting Notified About New Members

When someone subscribes to one of your membership plans, you'll receive a push notification. Tapping it takes you directly to that member's details.

Make sure **New Member** notifications are enabled in Profile → Notification Preferences.
