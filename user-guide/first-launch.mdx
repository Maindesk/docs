The first time you sign in to Your App, a few things happen to set up your experience. Here's what to expect so nothing comes as a surprise.

## Push Notification Permission

Shortly after signing in for the first time, your phone will show a system prompt asking if you'd like to receive notifications from Your App.

<Tip>
  Tap **Allow**. Push notifications are one of the most valuable features in the app, they alert you the moment a new order arrives, a booking is made, or a lead submits a form. Without them, you'd have to check the app manually to know anything happened.
</Tip>

If you accidentally tap "Don't Allow," you can enable notifications later:

- **iPhone:** Settings → Your App → Notifications → Allow Notifications
- **Android:** Settings → Apps → Your App → Notifications → toggle on

## Your Dashboard

After signing in and setting permissions, you land on the **Dashboard**, your business at a glance. You'll see stat cards for Revenue, Orders, Contacts, and Bookings, plus recent activity below.

## Navigating the App

**The side menu (hamburger menu):** Tap the three horizontal lines in the top-left corner to open the navigation menu. This is how you move between sections, Dashboard, Orders, Contacts, Bookings, Forms, Products, and more.

**The website selector:** If your account has multiple websites or funnels, you'll see a website selector at the top of the screen. Tap it to switch between your websites, each one shows its own independent data.

**The notification bell:** Tap the bell icon in the top-right corner to see your recent notifications. A red badge on the bell means there are unread notifications.

## What to Do First

1. **Check your dashboard** : get a feel for how your data is presented.
2. **Open the side menu** and explore the sections available to you.
3. **Set your notification preferences** : navigate to Profile → Notification Preferences and make sure the alerts that matter to you are turned on.
4. **Try the website selector** if you have multiple websites, switch between them and see how the data changes.

<Warning>
  If your dashboard shows zeros or empty lists, it's likely because you haven't connected a website yet, or your account is new. Check with your platform administrator if you expect to see data but don't.
</Warning>

## You're Ready

That's it. Your App is set up and ready to use. The rest of this guide covers each section in detail, start with whatever is most relevant to your business.
