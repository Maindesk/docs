The Contacts section is your full CRM, every customer, lead, and subscriber in one searchable, filterable list. Whether you're following up with a lead, checking a client's order history before a call, or adding someone you just met, it's all here.

## What You'll See

**Contact List**

Every contact in your platform account, displayed as cards showing:

- Full name
- Email address
- Tags (small colored labels indicating segments like "Lead," "VIP," or "Newsletter")

**Search Bar**

Type any name or email at the top to instantly filter the list.

**Tag Filter Pills**

Filter pills below the search bar let you tap a tag to show only contacts with that label. This is useful for quickly accessing a specific group, all VIPs, all leads, everyone on a specific list.

**Contact Detail Page**

Tap any contact to open their full profile:

- Name, email, phone number
- All assigned tags
- Subscriber list memberships
- Complete order history (every purchase they've made)

## How to Use Contacts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Contacts">
    Open the side menu and tap **Contacts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse or search">
    Scroll the list to browse, or type in the search bar to find a specific person by name or email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by tag">
    Tap a tag pill at the top to narrow the list to contacts with that tag.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a contact">
    Tap any contact card to see their full profile, tags, and order history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new contact">
    Tap the **\+** button in the top-right corner, fill in the contact details, and tap Save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Before a client call or meeting, pull up their contact card to check their recent purchases and notes. It takes 10 seconds and makes the conversation much more productive.
</Tip>

## Adding New Contacts

You can add contacts directly from the app:

1. Tap the **\+** button in the top-right corner of the Contacts screen.
2. Enter the contact's name, email address, and phone number.
3. Add any relevant tags.
4. Tap **Save**.

The new contact is immediately saved to your platform and synced across all devices.

<Warning>
  Contacts added in the app appear in your platform account instantly. There is no undo, if you add a contact by mistake, you'll need to delete them from your platform's web interface.
</Warning>
