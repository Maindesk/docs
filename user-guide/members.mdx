The Members section shows everyone who has an active membership subscription, giving you a comprehensive view of your subscriber base with filtering by group or plan.

## What You'll See

**Member List**

All active members displayed as cards with:

- Member name and profile photo (if available)
- Email address
- Membership plan name
- Join date
- Subscription status

**Group Filter**

Filter members by group or membership plan to focus on a specific segment of your subscriber base. Useful when you have multiple plans and want to view each independently.

## How to Use Members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Members">
    Open the side menu and tap **Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse your member list">
    Scroll through all active members. Each card shows their plan and join date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by group">
    Tap the group filter at the top to show only members on a specific plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a member for details">
    Tap any member card to see their full profile, including contact information and subscription history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use the group filter before a member-exclusive event or communication to pull up a clean list of everyone in that tier, handy for quickly counting attendees or preparing outreach.
</Tip>

## Members vs. Memberships

- **Memberships** : shows your plans (what you're selling) and their subscriber counts
- **Members** : shows the individual people subscribed to those plans

Both sections work together, check Memberships for a plan-level overview, and Members for a people-level view.
