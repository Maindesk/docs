The Bookings section shows all your scheduled appointments — past, present, and upcoming — organized with clear status labels and expandable detail cards so you're always prepared.

## What You'll See

**Status Filter Pills**

Three status categories at the top of the screen:
- **Upcoming** — future appointments that haven't happened yet
- **Today** — appointments scheduled for today
- **Completed** — past appointments

Tap any status pill to focus on that group.

**Date Filters**

Use the date filter to narrow bookings to a specific period — for example, next week or last month.

**Booking Cards**

Each booking appears as a card showing:
- Client name
- Service or appointment type
- Date and time
- Status label

**Expanded Booking Detail**

Tap any booking card to expand it and see the full details:
- Client contact information (email, phone)
- Service description
- Location or notes
- Any custom fields collected during booking

## How to Use Bookings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Bookings">
    Open the side menu and tap **Bookings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Today's appointments">
    Tap the **Today** filter pill to see only today's schedule — perfect for a morning briefing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View upcoming bookings">
    Tap **Upcoming** to see everything scheduled in the future.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a booking for details">
    Tap any booking card to expand it and see the full client and appointment information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the date filter for a specific period">
    Tap the date filter to narrow bookings to a specific week or month.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The **Today** filter is your best friend for a morning check — see exactly what appointments are coming up before you leave the house. Tap each one to see the client's contact info in case you need to reach them.
</Tip>

## Getting Notified About New Bookings

When a new appointment is booked through your website, you'll receive a push notification immediately. Tapping the notification opens Your App directly on that booking's detail.

Make sure **New Booking** notifications are enabled in Profile → Notification Preferences.
