Your App supports 43 languages. Changing the language switches the entire app interface instantly — every label, button, menu, and message — to your chosen language.

## How to Change the Language

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Profile">
    Tap the side menu icon, then tap **Profile** at the bottom of the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Language Preferences">
    Scroll down and tap **Language Preferences**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your language">
    Browse or search the list of 43 languages and tap the one you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The app switches instantly">
    The entire interface updates to your selected language immediately. No restart needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/user-guide/language-settings.png" alt="Language Preferences screen showing a searchable list of 43 supported languages" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
  Your language preference is saved permanently. Every time you open the app, it uses the language you selected — you only need to set it once.
</Tip>

## Supported Languages

Your App includes professional translations for all 43 of these languages:

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hebrew, Turkish, Polish, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish, Czech, Slovak, Hungarian, Romanian, Bulgarian, Croatian, Serbian, Ukrainian, Greek, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Malay, Hindi, Bengali, Urdu, Persian (Farsi), Catalan, Basque, Galician, Lithuanian, Latvian, Estonian

## What Gets Translated

Switching the language changes:
- All navigation labels (menu items, section names)
- All button text
- All form labels and placeholder text
- All system messages and alerts
- All notification text within the app

Your actual business data — order details, contact names, product descriptions — is not translated, as that content comes directly from your platform and is outside the app's translation scope.
