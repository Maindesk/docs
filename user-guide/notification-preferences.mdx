Your App lets you control each notification type individually — so you get the alerts that matter to your business without being overwhelmed by ones that don't.

## How to Access Notification Preferences

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Profile">
    Tap the side menu icon, then tap **Profile** at the bottom of the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Notification Preferences">
    Scroll down and tap **Notification Preferences**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle each notification type">
    Each notification type has an on/off toggle. Green means it's enabled — you'll receive these notifications. Grey means it's disabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Changes save automatically">
    There's no Save button — your preferences update immediately when you toggle a switch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Notification Toggles Explained

| Toggle | What It Controls |
|--------|-----------------|
| **New Order** | Push notification when a customer places an order |
| **New Contact** | Push notification when a new contact is added |
| **New Booking** | Push notification when a new appointment is scheduled |
| **Form Submission** | Push notification when a visitor submits a form |
| **New Member** | Push notification when someone subscribes to a membership plan |

<Tip>
  We recommend keeping **New Order**, **New Booking**, and **Form Submission** turned on. These are time-sensitive events where fast response directly impacts your revenue.
</Tip>

## System-Level Notification Settings

Notification preferences in the app control which types of alerts Your App sends. But your phone's system settings control whether any notifications appear at all.

If your preferences are set correctly in the app but you're still not receiving notifications, check:

- **iPhone:** Settings → Your App → Notifications → Allow Notifications must be ON
- **Android:** Settings → Apps → Your App → Notifications must be ON

Both need to be enabled for notifications to arrive.

<Warning>
  If you sign out of Your App, push notifications stop until you sign back in. This is intentional — notifications are linked to your active session.
</Warning>
