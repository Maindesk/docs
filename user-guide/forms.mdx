The Forms section shows every submission from contact forms, inquiry forms, quote requests, and any other forms on your website. New submissions are clearly flagged so nothing gets missed.

## What You'll See

**Submission Cards**

Each form submission appears as a card showing:

- The form name (so you know which form on your site was filled out)
- Submitter name or email
- Date and time of submission
- An **unread indicator**, a blue dot and **NEW** pill for submissions you haven't viewed yet

**Expanded Submission Detail**

Tap any submission card to see every field the visitor filled out, name, email, phone number, message, and any custom fields your form includes.

**Read/Unread Tracking**

Submissions you haven't opened yet are highlighted with a blue dot. Once you tap to open a submission, it's marked as read automatically. The unread count shown in the navigation decreases accordingly.

## How to Use Forms

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Forms">
    Open the side menu and tap **Forms**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check for new submissions">
    Submissions with a blue dot and NEW pill are unread, address these first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a submission to read it">
    Tap any card to expand it and see everything the visitor entered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up while it's fresh">
    Copy the submitter's phone number or email directly from the expanded view to follow up immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The faster you follow up on a form submission, the more likely you are to convert that lead. Responding within a few minutes — when the person is still at their computer or phone, is dramatically more effective than responding hours later.
</Tip>

## Getting Notified About New Submissions

When someone fills out a form on your website, you'll receive a push notification immediately. Tapping it opens Your App directly on that submission's detail.

Make sure **Form Submission** notifications are enabled in Profile → Notification Preferences.

<Warning>
  If you see a lot of spam submissions, that's something to address on your website (by adding CAPTCHA or spam filtering to your forms). The Forms section in the app shows all submissions exactly as they arrive from your platform.
</Warning>
