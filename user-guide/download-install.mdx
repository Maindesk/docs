Your App is available on both the Apple App Store and Google Play. Installation takes less than two minutes.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iOS (iPhone & iPad)">
    **System Requirements:** iOS 14 or later

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the App Store">
        On your iPhone or iPad, tap the **App Store** icon on your home screen.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Search for the app">
        Tap the **Search** tab at the bottom, then type the name of your app in the search bar.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap the app in results">
        Find your app in the search results — look for your company's name listed as the developer.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap Get">
        Tap **Get** (or the download icon if you've installed it before). You may be asked for your Apple ID password or Face ID/Touch ID to confirm.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Wait for installation">
        The app downloads and installs automatically. When the button changes to **Open**, it's ready.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the app">
        Tap **Open** in the App Store, or find the app icon on your home screen.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      If you can't find the app by name, ask your platform administrator for the direct App Store link.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    **System Requirements:** Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Google Play">
        On your Android phone or tablet, tap the **Google Play** icon.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Search for the app">
        Tap the search bar at the top and type the name of your app.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap the app in results">
        Find your app in the results — check that your company name appears as the developer.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap Install">
        Tap **Install**. The app will download and install automatically.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the app">
        Tap **Open** in the Play Store, or find the app icon in your app drawer or home screen.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      If you can't find the app by name, ask your platform administrator for the direct Google Play link.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## After Installation

Once the app is installed, you're ready to sign in. Your App uses the same login credentials as your website platform account — you don't need to create a new account.

[Continue to Signing In →](/user-guide/signing-in)

<Warning>
  Make sure you're downloading the correct app from the correct developer. Your platform administrator can give you the exact App Store or Google Play link to avoid any confusion.
</Warning>
