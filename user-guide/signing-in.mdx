Your App uses the same email address and password as your existing platform account. There's nothing new to remember, if you can log in to your platform on a computer, you can log in to the app.

## How to Sign In

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your App">
    Tap the app icon on your home screen to launch it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    Type the email address you use to log in to your platform account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your password">
    Type your password. Tap the eye icon to reveal it if you need to check what you're typing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Sign In">
    Tap the **Sign In** button. If your credentials are correct, you'll be taken directly to your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Forgot Your Password?

If you can't remember your password:

1. Tap **Forgot Password?** on the sign-in screen.
2. Enter your email address and tap **Send Reset Link**.
3. Check your inbox for a password reset email.
4. Follow the link in the email to set a new password.
5. Return to Your App and sign in with the new password.

<Tip>
  The password reset email sometimes lands in your spam or junk folder. If you don't see it within a few minutes, check there.
</Tip>

## New to the Platform?

If you haven't created an account on the platform yet:

1. Visit your platform's website on a browser (desktop or mobile).
2. Sign up for a free account or trial.
3. Once your account is created, return to Your App and sign in with those credentials.

<Warning>
  Your App requires an existing platform account. You cannot create a new account from within the app — sign up on the website first.
</Warning>

## Staying Signed In

Your App keeps you signed in automatically. You only need to sign in again if you explicitly sign out or if your session expires for security reasons.
