The Subscriber Lists section shows all your email marketing lists, with open and click rate metrics for each one, so you can track engagement and manage list organization from your phone.

## What You'll See

**List Cards**

Each subscriber list displayed with:

- List name
- Total subscriber count
- Open rate (percentage of recipients who opened recent emails)
- Click rate (percentage who clicked a link in recent emails)

**List Actions**

Tap a list to see:

- Full subscriber count
- Performance metrics
- Option to rename the list

## How to Use Subscriber Lists

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Subscriber Lists">
    Open the side menu and tap **Subscriber Lists**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your lists">
    See all your active lists with their subscriber counts and engagement rates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a list">
    Tap any list card to see full details and access the rename option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rename a list">
    Inside the list detail, tap **Rename**, enter the new name, and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Good open and click rates vary by industry, but as a general benchmark, 20–30% open rates and 2–5% click rates are solid for email marketing. If a list is significantly below these, it may be worth reviewing who's on it or how recently they signed up.
</Tip>

## Understanding the Metrics

**Open Rate** : the percentage of people who opened an email sent to this list. A higher open rate generally means the list has engaged, interested subscribers.

**Click Rate** : the percentage of recipients who clicked a link inside the email. This indicates how compelling your content and calls-to-action are.

<Warning>
  Subscriber list management, adding, removing, or exporting subscribers, must be done through your platform's web dashboard. The app shows read-only list data and allows renaming only.
</Warning>
