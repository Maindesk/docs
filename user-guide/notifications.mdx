Your App sends push notifications for every important business event — new orders, bookings, leads, messages, and more. This section explains where to see them and what each notification means.

## The Notification Bell

The bell icon in the top-right corner of the app opens your notification history — a list of all recent alerts. A red badge on the bell shows how many unread notifications you have.

Tap the bell to open the notification list. Tap any notification in the list to navigate directly to the relevant content.

## Tapping a Notification

Whether you tap a notification from your phone's lock screen or from the in-app notification list, Your App responds the same way:

- It opens the app (if it wasn't already open)
- It switches to the correct website context (if the event belongs to a specific website)
- It navigates directly to the relevant page and record

For example:
- Tapping a **New Order** notification opens that specific order's detail page
- Tapping a **New Booking** notification opens that specific booking's detail
- Tapping a **Form Submission** notification opens that submission and marks it as read

## Notification Types

| Emoji | Notification | What It Means |
|-------|-------------|---------------|
| 🛒 | **New Order** | A customer placed an order on your website |
| 👤 | **New Contact** | A new contact was added to your CRM |
| 📅 | **New Booking** | A new appointment was scheduled |
| 📋 | **Form Submission** | A visitor filled out a form on your website |
| 🏷️ | **New Member** | Someone subscribed to one of your membership plans |
