The Sales & Orders section shows you every order placed through your website, with a revenue chart, date filters, and full order details, all in real time.

## What You'll See

**Revenue Chart**

A visual chart at the top of the screen showing your earnings over the selected time period. Use this to spot trends: are sales growing, steady, or dipping?

**Date Filter Pills**

Filter buttons below the chart for quick period selection:

- **Today** : just today's orders and revenue
- **7 Days** : the last seven days
- **30 Days** : the last 30 days
- **All** : your complete order history
- **Custom** : choose any specific start and end date

**Order Cards**

Each order appears as a card showing:

- Customer name and email
- Order amount
- Date and time
- Status pill (color-coded): **Pending**, **Processing**, **Completed**, **Cancelled**, or **Refunded**

**Order Details**

Tap any order card to expand it and see:

- Full line items (products, quantities, prices)
- Customer contact information
- Shipping or delivery address
- Payment method

## How to Use Sales & Orders

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Sales & Orders">
    Open the side menu and tap **Sales & Orders**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a date range">
    Tap one of the filter pills, Today, 7 Days, 30 Days, All, or Custom, to set the period you want to view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the revenue chart">
    The chart updates to show revenue for your selected period. Look for peaks and trends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse order cards">
    Scroll through the order list below the chart to see individual transactions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap an order for details">
    Tap any order card to expand it and see the full breakdown, items, customer info, and more.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use the **Custom** date range to pull a specific period, for example, orders from a promotional campaign week, so you can measure results precisely.
</Tip>

## Getting Notified About New Orders

You'll receive a push notification the moment a new order is placed. Tapping the notification opens Your App directly on that order's detail, no navigation needed.

Make sure **New Order** notifications are enabled in Profile → Notification Preferences.

<Warning>
  If you're not receiving new order notifications, check that notifications are enabled both in the app (Profile → Notification Preferences) and in your phone's system settings (Settings → Your App → Notifications).
</Warning>
