The Profile section is where you manage your account settings, get help, and sign out of Your App.

## How to Access Profile

Tap the side menu icon (the three horizontal lines in the top-left corner), then tap **Profile** at the bottom of the navigation menu.

## What You'll Find

**Account Information**

Your name and email address, confirming which account you're signed into.

**Notification Preferences**

Control which push notifications you receive. See [Notification Preferences](/user-guide/notification-preferences) for a full guide.

**Language Preferences**

Change the app's display language. See [Language Settings](/user-guide/language-settings) for a full guide.

**Contact Us / Support**

Tap **Contact Us** or **Support** to reach out for help. This opens a contact form, email link, or support chat depending on how your platform has configured it.

**Sign Out**

Tap **Sign Out** to log out of Your App.

## Signing Out

To sign out:

1. Open the side menu.
2. Tap **Profile**.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Sign Out**.
4. Confirm the sign-out when prompted.

<Warning>
  When you sign out, push notifications stop immediately. You will not receive any alerts for new orders, bookings, or other events until you sign back in. If you share a device with others, make sure you're comfortable with this before signing out.
</Warning>

## Getting Support

If something isn't working or you have a question:

1. Tap **Contact Us** in the Profile section.
2. Describe your issue or question.
3. Your platform's support team will respond.

For issues with the app itself that your platform's support team can't resolve, they may escalate to Pixel Punch on your behalf.
