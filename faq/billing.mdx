<Accordion title="Do I need Apple and Google developer accounts?">
  Yes. Publishing an app to the App Store and Google Play requires a developer account with each platform. This is a standard requirement for any app in any store, regardless of who builds it.

  - **Apple Developer Program** — [developer.apple.com](https://developer.apple.com/programs/)
  - **Google Play Console** — [play.google.com/console](https://play.google.com/console/)

  See the [Onboarding Checklist →](/product/onboarding) for step-by-step instructions on setting up both accounts.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How much do Apple and Google charge?">
  These fees are paid directly to Apple and Google, not through Pixel Punch:

  | Platform | Fee | Type |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Apple Developer Program | \$99/year | Annual renewal |
  | Google Play Developer Account | \$25 | One-time registration |

  Apple's fee renews annually. Google's is a one-time payment. Both are standard costs for any business with apps in their stores.

  <Note>
    These fees apply to **every** app on their stores. They are not specific to Pixel Punch or white-label apps, they're the cost of having an app in the store at all.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What does the setup fee cover?">
  The setup fee covers:

  - Full white-label customization of the app (name, icon, colors, splash screen, branding)
  - Firebase project setup and configuration (your data, your project)
  - API and webhook integration with your platform
  - Push notification certificate setup for iOS and Android
  - Internal testing and QA
  - TestFlight and Google Play beta testing coordination
  - App Store and Google Play listing preparation (screenshots, description, keywords)
  - Submission and management of both store review processes
  - Launch coordination and handoff

  In short: everything needed to go from "I want an app" to "my app is live in the stores under my brand."
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What does the monthly fee include?">
  The monthly fee covers:

  - Over-the-air (OTA) updates, all new features and improvements delivered automatically
  - iOS and Android OS compatibility maintenance (when Apple or Google release new OS versions, we ensure compatibility)
  - Bug fixes and technical support
  - Infrastructure costs (Firebase, Google Cloud)
  - Access to all new features as they're released

  Your app improves over time at no additional cost, every improvement Pixel Punch ships goes to all white-label partners.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I cancel?">
  If you decide to cancel your Pixel Punch subscription:

  - The app remains in the App Store and Google Play under your accounts, it doesn't disappear
  - OTA updates and new features will no longer be delivered
  - Your Firebase project and data remain yours, you keep your data
  - Your Apple and Google developer accounts remain active (those are yours independently)

  We recommend reaching out before cancelling, many concerns that lead to cancellation are addressable, and we'd rather solve the problem than lose a partner.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I offer the app to my users for free?">
  Yes. How you price the app to your users is entirely your decision. Many partners include it as a standard feature for all users, positioning it as a differentiator that makes their platform more valuable than competitors. Others include it only in higher-tier plans as an upgrade incentive, or charge a small monthly add-on fee.

  Your pricing model to your users does not affect your Pixel Punch pricing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if I don't have an Apple Developer account yet?">
  No problem, you can start the Pixel Punch onboarding process before your Apple Developer account is approved. Apple's enrollment review typically takes 24–48 hours.

  We recommend starting the Apple enrollment process as the very first step, since it's the only part of the process that has a waiting period outside of your or our control. Everything else can proceed in parallel.

  See the [Apple Developer Account setup guide →](/product/onboarding) for step-by-step instructions.
</Accordion>
