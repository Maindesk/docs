<Accordion title="What features are included?">
  Every white-label app includes the full feature set — there are no add-on tiers or feature unlocks. Here's what's included:

  **For your users:**
  - Dashboard (revenue, orders, contacts, bookings at a glance)
  - Sales & Orders (real-time order tracking with revenue chart and date filters)
  - Contacts & CRM (full customer database with tag filtering and order history)
  - Bookings (appointment management with status and date filters)
  - Form Submissions (lead capture with unread tracking)
  - Products (catalog with QR code sharing)
  - Memberships (plans and subscriber management with QR sharing)
  - Push Notifications (instant alerts for every business event)
  - Live Traffic Analytics (powered by Google Analytics 4)
  - Live Chat (real-time messaging with website visitors)
  - 43 languages built in

  **For you (admin):**
  - Admin Dashboard (live visitor map, platform revenue, users, trials)
  - Users Management
  - Projects Management and Activation
  - Push Campaigns (targeted broadcast notifications)
  - News & Announcements
  - Automation Rules
  - Admin push notifications

  See the [Features Overview →](/product/features-overview) for the full picture.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can my users manage their websites from the app?">
  The app focuses on business management — sales, contacts, bookings, forms, products, and memberships — rather than website editing. Users can view and track everything happening in their business, receive real-time notifications, and manage customer relationships.

  Website building and editing remains on the desktop platform. The app is designed to be what users need while they're away from their computer — not a replacement for the full platform experience.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do push notifications work?">
  Push notifications are delivered through Apple's notification infrastructure (APNs) for iOS devices and Google's infrastructure (FCM) for Android devices. When an event occurs on a user's website or account — a new order, booking, form submission, etc. — a notification is sent to their device within seconds.

  Users can control which notification types they receive in the app's notification preferences. Notifications include a preview of the relevant content (customer name, order amount, etc.) and navigate directly to the relevant record when tapped.

  See [Push Notifications →](/product/push-notifications) for the complete guide.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is Live Chat?">
  Live Chat lets platform admins have real-time conversations with visitors on their users' websites. When a visitor types a message in a chat widget, the admin receives a push notification and can reply directly from the app.

  Chat conversations are organized by status (Open, Awaiting, Closed) and update in real time. This requires a connected LiveChat workspace — it's optional and can be set up at any time.

  See [Live Chat →](/product/live-chat) for details.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is Traffic Analytics?">
  Traffic Analytics is a real-time and historical view of website visitor data, powered by Google Analytics 4. Admins and users can see live visitors on a world map, current page views, device breakdown, traffic sources, top pages, and historical trends.

  This requires a Google Analytics 4 property connected to the website. It's optional and can be added at any time after launch.

  See [Live Traffic Analytics →](/product/live-analytics) for details.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I send marketing campaigns to my users?">
  Yes. The admin section includes a full push campaign tool — you can write a message, select an audience (all users, active subscribers, trial users, or other segments), set a deep link, and send immediately or save as a draft.

  You can also set up automation rules for triggered messages (e.g., a reminder 3 days before a trial expires) and publish news articles delivered to users' apps.

  See [Marketing Campaigns →](/product/marketing-campaigns) for details.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a limit on users or projects?">
  No. There are no per-user or per-project limits. Whether your platform has 50 users or 50,000, the app handles it. The underlying infrastructure (Firebase and Google Cloud) scales automatically.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can my users use the app for free?">
  That's entirely up to you. You decide how to price and offer the app to your users. Common approaches include:
  - Including it in all plans as a standard feature
  - Including it only in premium plans as an upgrade incentive
  - Offering it as a paid add-on for any plan

  Pixel Punch charges you (the platform operator) for the app. How you monetize it to your users is your business decision.
</Accordion>
