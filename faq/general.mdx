<Accordion title="What is this app?">
  Your App is a fully branded mobile application that your website builder platform offers to its users. It's available on the Apple App Store and Google Play under your company's name, and it lets your users manage their entire business from their phone — checking orders, responding to leads, tracking bookings, reviewing analytics, and more.

  It's built and maintained by Pixel Punch, but every user-facing element carries your brand. Your users see your company, not ours.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who is the app for?">
  The app serves two audiences:

  **Your platform users** — the business owners, freelancers, and entrepreneurs who use your website builder platform. They use the app to manage their day-to-day business activity: sales, contacts, bookings, forms, memberships, and analytics.

  **You (the platform operator)** — you use the admin section of the app to manage your platform: user and project management, push campaigns, live chat, analytics, and subscription management.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is it really my brand? Will my users know it's from Pixel Punch?">
  Completely your brand. Your users will not see any Pixel Punch branding anywhere.

  The app is published in the App Store and Google Play under **your** developer account, with **your** company name as the developer. The app name, icon, splash screen, colors, and all branding reflect your identity.

  When users search for the app, download it, or open it, they see your brand exclusively. Pixel Punch provides the technology infrastructure — your users experience your product.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do my users get the app?">
  You share the App Store and Google Play links with your users — via email, in-app message, on your website, or in your onboarding flow. Users search for your app by name on their device or tap the link you provide, download it, and sign in with their existing platform credentials.

  There's no separate registration required. If they already have an account on your platform, they can sign in to the app immediately.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What languages are supported?">
  Your App includes professional translations for 43 languages, built in and available from day one:

  English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hebrew, Turkish, Polish, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish, Czech, Slovak, Hungarian, Romanian, Bulgarian, Croatian, Serbian, Ukrainian, Greek, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Malay, Hindi, Bengali, Urdu, Persian (Farsi), Catalan, Basque, Galician, Lithuanian, Latvian, Estonian.

  Users select their preferred language in the app settings. No translation work is required from you.
</Accordion>
